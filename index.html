Black Static

Welcome to Black Static — a corner of the internet built for those of us who never look away from the screen.

This site was created by a lifelong horror fan for other horror fans — the kind who stay through the credits, who argue about practical effects versus CGI, who know that tension is better than jump scares, and who understand that the best horror lingers long after the lights come back on. Whether your heart belongs to slashers, psychological thrillers, cosmic dread, or underground indie nightmares, you’re in the right place.

Black Static is a space to explore, analyze, recommend, and obsess. Expect reviews that dig deeper than surface-level scares, discussions about symbolism and storytelling, and appreciation for the artists who push boundaries in the dark.

Stay curious. Stay uncomfortable. And keep watching.

Genres

Slasher

A staple of horror cinema, the slasher genre features a ruthless killer, a group of unsuspecting victims, and a deadly game of survival. With iconic villains and unforgettable chase scenes, these films define fear.

Apocalyptic/Zombie

Zombie films explore survival, infection, collapse of society, and humanity under pressure. From slow-burn dread to action-packed chaos, the undead remain a horror staple.

Paranormal

Paranormal horror focuses on ghosts, hauntings, and supernatural forces. These stories build tension through atmosphere, mystery, and unseen threats.

Found Footage

Told through lost tapes and shaky cameras, Found Footage blurs the line between fiction and reality — making every moment feel dangerously authentic.
